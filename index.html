Typing Test

Goal: 35–40 WPM with ≥95% accuracy

🖥️

Use a Desktop / Laptop

A physical keyboard gives you the speed and consistency needed to hit your WPM target.

🎯

Focus on Accuracy First

Slow down slightly to avoid mistakes. Errors hurt more than a few extra seconds.

🔥

Warm Up Online

Practice on typing sites for 10–15 minutes before the real assessment to loosen up.

🤫

Find a Quiet Environment

Distractions cause typos. Sit somewhere comfortable and turn off notifications.

2

Logical Reasoning

Tests problem-solving and pattern recognition

Common Question Types

🔷

Shapes / Dots / Circles

Identify the next item in a sequence. Watch for rotation, shading, and the number of elements.

🔢

Number Sequences

Look for addition/subtraction, multiplication, squares, or alternating patterns.

🔄

Rotations & Reflections

Shapes may rotate clockwise/counter-clockwise or flip — isolate one feature at a time.

Tips

  • Observe carefully: check rotation, size, shading, and count of elements.
  • Break patterns down: analyze one feature (e.g., position, color) at a time.
  • Manage your time: skip hard items and come back to them later.
3

Written Language & Reading Passage

Assesses grammar, comprehension, and interpretation

Key Tips

  • Read all instructions carefully before starting.
  • For the final passage, read the entire text first, then answer.
  • Highlight keywords and identify the main idea of each paragraph.
  • Expect questions on inference, tone, and vocabulary in context.
  • Double-check grammar and spelling in any written responses.

📝 Pro Tip

When answering inference questions, the correct answer is supported by the text — it isn't just a true-sounding opinion. Always find the sentence that backs up your choice.
